FoundTogether

This is a working prototype (demo). Oak Grove is a sample town, and all its places, people and items are made up. Anything you enter is saved only on your own device and is never sent anywhere.

Try it here: https://imk-8.github.io/FoundTogether/

How to use it

	1.	Report something you lost. On the Home screen, tap the type of item, then follow the four steps: what it looks like, when you lost it, every place you went, and one private detail only you would know.
	2.	See your matches. FoundTogether checks every partner place at once and shows each possible match with a score out of 100 and the reasons behind it.
	3.	Claim it. Tap "This might be mine" on a match.
	4.	Switch to Staff. Use the Resident and Staff switch at the top. In Claims, staff compare your private details with their notes and tap Approve.
	5.	Pick it up. Switch back to Resident and open My reports to see your pickup code. Then switch to Staff and enter the code under "Owner at the desk?" to release the item.
	6.	Explore. Browse shows found items, the Network tab shows the town map and stats, and Reset demo at the bottom of the Network tab starts fresh.
